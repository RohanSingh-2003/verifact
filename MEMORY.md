## 1. What is VeriFact?
VeriFact is a dual-pipeline, explainable AI hallucination detection system. It analyzes answers generated by Large Language Models using:
1. **Reference-Free Metamorphic Consistency (MetaQA)**: Perturbation testing (synonyms, antonyms, negations) based on Yang et al. (*FSE 2025*).
2. **External Factual Grounding (Web Evidence)**: Atomic claim extraction, multi-pass Tavily search, 6-tier domain authority ranking, and relevance filtering based on ClaimCheck (*ACL 2025*).
3. **Direct-Answer Multi-Model Peer Review**: Concurrent review by independent models detecting contradictions.

**Current Status**: Feature-complete, quality-audited, release freeze.

## 3. Critical Scoring Formulas & Constraints

1. **MetaQA Score (Yang et al. Eq. 5)**:
   $$S = \frac{1}{2} \left( \frac{1}{|M_{\text{syn}}|} \sum \text{penalty}(m) + \frac{1}{|M_{\text{ant}}|} \sum \text{penalty}(m) \right)$$
   - Fixed Threshold: $\theta = 0.50$ (Score $\ge 0.50 \rightarrow$ Hallucinated; $< 0.50 \rightarrow$ Reliable). **Never modify this formula or threshold.**
2. **Web Evidence Score**:
   $$\text{Consistency Score} = \text{Average contribution of assessed claims}$$
   - Supported = `1.0`, Insufficient Evidence = `0.5`, Contradicted = `0.0`.
   - `NOT_ASSESSED_NO_RELEVANT_SOURCE` claims are **excluded from the calculation**.
3. **Metric Independence**: **NEVER blend the Web Evidence score into the MetaQA score.** They are orthogonal signals.
4. **Epistemic Rule**: Absence of online evidence is not proof of falsehood. Failed verifier API calls are marked `FAILED`, not negative verdicts.
